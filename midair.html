Designing under observation.

This assessment explored how designers respond to AI-moderated hiring scenarios. By participating in and documenting the process, the research surfaces what AI evaluators prioritise, where they fall short, and how designers can communicate their thinking more effectively in high-stakes, time-pressured environments.
